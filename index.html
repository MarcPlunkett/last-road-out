The Fight

Battles are real time on a 3×6 street grid. You hold the left nine tiles, the Outfit holds the right nine.

Every enemy attack is telegraphed in red. Damage lands only on the marked tiles, at the moment the warning fills. Be somewhere else.

Tricks & Planning

Tricks are powerful one-use moves drawn from your deck. Each battle opens with the Planning screen: five cards are drawn and you load any legal combination. They queue up in the order picked; K fires the next one.

The Planning Gauge fills over 8 seconds of fighting. When it reads PLAN READY, press C to draw a fresh hand (combat pauses while you choose). Unused loaded Tricks are discarded.

Code Rule

Every card has a code: A, B, C or wildcard *. A hand is legal when:

• every card shares one letter (* fits any letter), or
• every card has the same name.

Quick Shot A + Dynamite A ✓   Quick Shot A + B ✓
Quick Shot A + Smoke * ✓   Quick Shot A + Switchblade B ✗

Aimed Tricks

Dynamite and Molotov need a target: time slows, click an enemy tile (or press K again for the best spot). Esc or right-click cancels without spending the card.

The Road Map

Each road is a branching map of four stops and a boss. Choose any stop connected to where you stand.

Battle — reward: cash, a Trick, or recovery.
Elite — a named lieutenant, better spoils.
Black Market — buy Tricks, healing, upgrades.
Safehouse — one free permanent upgrade.
Boss — guards the way to the next road.

HP, cash, deck and upgrades carry through the whole run. Die, and the run is over.

Tip: charge your shot while dodging, release when they line up.
